Scope: VAULT 876 landing page (Phase 1 shell). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: prospective salon clients, mostly on mobile, deciding whether to book or call.
Action: scroll to services and book (Phase 2) or call now. Proof/content: editorial imagery + named services. Constraints: binding palette (obsidian/white/gold), absolutely no pink, high-contrast serif + clean sans, React/Vite/Tailwind/Framer Motion/Router.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A hair salon rendered as a luxury fashion house with cultural swagger — the quiet authority of a Céline or Vogue cover fused with the warm, confident, unapologetic energy of a high-end American Black beauty salon (luxury Atlanta/Houston). Editorial elegance + Apple-grade smoothness + cultural confidence. It refuses the category default: the cluttered promo flyer, the service laundry-list, the hot-pink "glam" accent, same-size icon cards. One idea it owns: editorial restraint, warmed by cultural voice, as the signal of expertise.

OWN-WORLD: Matte obsidian ground (#0A0A0A) held nearly empty. Crisp white type. Metallic gold (#D4AF37) stays precise on UI — primary actions and hairline accents — never a glow or gradient wash on chrome or content. Sanctioned atmosphere: a single faint, slow-drifting screen-blended gold aura in the obsidian ground (depth, not decoration), and a gold "gleam" that sweeps across a service row on hover. TYPE is an editorial contrast: warm high-contrast Didone display in Playfair Display, heavy poster-weight service names in Montserrat ExtraBold uppercase, UI/body in Inter. Full-bleed editorial photography of Black hair and beauty (currently branded placeholders until the salon's own shoot). Hairline 1px gold/white dividers. Generous whitespace is the primary material. Motion settles on one house spring — slow, smooth, a touch of swagger — never a cartoon bounce.

STORY: Visitor lands in a calm, expensive-feeling dark room, reads one confident line ("Flawless. Every time."), understands this is premium beauty for all hair types, and either scrolls into the six services or calls/books immediately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen obsidian hero. Large Playfair display headline centered, small Inter subhead beneath, actions below (gold-filled "Book Now" + ghost "Call Salon"; on mobile Call Salon only, with a persistent sticky Book Now bar). A full-bleed branded medallion field anchors the background at scale. Glass navbar pinned top: wordmark left, links + gold "Book Now" right.

FORM: Editorial full-bleed hero over a quiet six-up service grid — position 1 of the ordered structures; seed: none (pinned). Pinned by the client's words: "Take the luxury black-and-gold branding of Vault 876, but execute it with the extreme minimalism and clean UX of the HeyGoldie site", "Matte black background (#0A0A0A), white text, and gold (#D4AF37)", "ABSOLUTELY NO PINK", "large, editorial, magazine-style photos", "massive negative space", and "Apple makes a Black urban hair and beauty salon."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
