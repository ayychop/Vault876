Scope: VAULT 876 landing page (Phase 1 shell). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: prospective salon clients, mostly on mobile, deciding whether to book or call.
Action: scroll to services and book (Phase 2) or call now. Proof/content: editorial imagery + named services. Constraints: binding palette (obsidian/white/gold), absolutely no pink, high-contrast serif + clean sans, React/Vite/Tailwind/Framer Motion/Router.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A hair salon rendered as a luxury fashion house — the quiet authority of a Céline or Vogue cover, not a salon price-list. It refuses the category default: the cluttered promo flyer, the service laundry-list, the hot-pink "glam" accent, same-size icon cards. One idea it owns: editorial restraint as the signal of expertise.

OWN-WORLD: Matte obsidian ground (#0A0A0A) held nearly empty. Crisp white type. Metallic gold (#D4AF37) only on primary actions and hairline accents — a precious metal, never a glow or gradient wash. Display set in Bodoni Moda (Didone, high stroke contrast — the fashion-magazine serif); UI/body in Inter. Full-bleed editorial photography of Black hair and beauty. Hairline 1px gold/white dividers. Generous whitespace is the primary material.

STORY: Visitor lands in a calm, expensive-feeling dark room, reads one confident line ("Look good. Feel good."), understands this is premium beauty for all hair types, and either scrolls into the six services or calls/books immediately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen obsidian hero. Large Bodoni display headline left/center, small Inter subhead beneath, two actions below (gold-filled "Call Salon" + ghost "Explore Services"). A single editorial portrait anchors the right/background at scale. Glass navbar pinned top: wordmark left, links + gold "Book Now" right.

FORM: Editorial full-bleed hero over a quiet six-up service grid — position 1 of the ordered structures; seed: none (pinned). Pinned by the client's words: "Take the luxury black-and-gold branding of Vault 876, but execute it with the extreme minimalism and clean UX of the HeyGoldie site", "Matte black background (#0A0A0A), white text, and gold (#D4AF37)", "ABSOLUTELY NO PINK", "large, editorial, magazine-style photos", "massive negative space", and "Apple makes a Black urban hair and beauty salon."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
