# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, React Router. Pinned by the client brief.

## Users

Primary: clients booking hair and beauty services at VAULT 876 — a Black-owned urban hair and beauty salon serving all hair types. They arrive from phone, Instagram, or word of mouth, often on mobile, and want to understand the salon's services and (from Phase 2) book an appointment without calling. Secondary: walk-in and call-in clients who currently reach the salon by phone or directions.

## Product Purpose

A premium salon website that moves VAULT 876 from an informational brochure (call / get directions only) to a bookable experience. Success = a visitor understands what the salon is and books, or calls, within seconds, and the brand reads as high-end rather than busy.

## Positioning

Luxury Black-and-gold salon branding executed with the extreme minimalism and clean booking UX of a modern booking app. The merge — fashion-house luxury + app-grade clarity — is the differentiator against both the cluttered incumbent and generic salon-booking tools.

## Operating Context

Phase 1 (this work): the landing page shell — Navbar, Hero, Services grid (6 services), Our Promise, Footer. Phase 2: a 3-step booking flow (Select service → Date & time → Enter details) with a mandatory sticky bottom bar showing running price total and a large Continue button, styled in the same dark-luxury aesthetic.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six services at launch: Wig Installations, Relaxers, Braid Extensions, Kids Hair Braiding, Eyelash Extensions, Locs.
- Fully responsive, mobile-first; animations must be smooth (Framer Motion).
- React Router in place; `/booking` route stubbed for Phase 2.
- Must compile and run via `npm run dev`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VAULT 876.
- Colors (binding): Matte Obsidian Black `#0A0A0A` ground, Crisp White `#FFFFFF` text, Metallic Gold `#D4AF37` for buttons and highlights.
- **Absolutely no pink** — the incumbent's out-of-place hot-pink accent is the thing being removed.
- Typography register: high-contrast serif for display headings, clean sans-serif for body/UI.
- Anti-reference (incumbent): heavy, cluttered, dark, a busy promotional flyer embedded as the page, hot-pink category labels. Replace the flyer with large editorial, magazine-style photography and massive negative space.
- Tone: luxury fashion brand, "Apple makes a Black urban hair and beauty salon."

## Product Principles

1. Negative space is the luxury — remove before adding.
2. Gold is a precious metal, not a glow: used sparingly on actions and accents, never as ambient decoration.
3. Every call to action is live and honest; no dead buttons.
4. Mobile-first, because most clients arrive on a phone.
5. The brand reads premium before it reads "salon."
